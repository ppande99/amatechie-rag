# Compensation bands (confidential)

Salary bands for engineering levels L3 to L7 are reviewed each January. Offers above band need
approval from the VP of People. This document must not be shared outside the HR group.

Current bands (annual, USD):

- L3: $95,000 to $120,000
- L4: $120,000 to $150,000
- L5: $150,000 to $185,000
- L6: $185,000 to $230,000
- L7: $230,000 to $290,000
