# Incident response policy

Incidents are classified Sev1 to Sev4. Sev1 means customer-facing outage or data exposure. The
incident commander is the first on-call engineer to acknowledge. A status update is posted every
thirty minutes during Sev1 and Sev2. A post-incident review is mandatory for Sev1 and Sev2 and is
completed within five business days.
