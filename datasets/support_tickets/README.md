# Synthetic support tickets (fictional company "Lumio")

Generated by `scripts/gen_tickets.py` (seeded, reproducible). No real customer data.

- `tickets.jsonl`: 196 tickets with gold labels (`category`, `priority`, `expected_action`, `refund_amount`, `requires_human`, `is_adversarial`, `known_issue`).
- `known_issues.json`: the fake known-issues tracker the agent can look up.

Built-in scenarios: a 24-minute outage spike (KI-130) for the incident watcher, 17 adversarial tickets (prompt injection, authority claims, PII fishing, exfiltration), vague tickets needing clarification, Spanish tickets, and 10 duplicates.

Refund policy used for labels: duplicate charges up to $100 can be proposed automatically; anything above $100, annual-plan refunds, security and privacy cases need a human.
